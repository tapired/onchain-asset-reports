# JOC strategy auction homework — fixed-block read-only assessment

> Publication copy of the November 1, 2026 fixed-block assessment. Values and role holders are historical snapshots. Supporting data has internal audit-server paths normalized; raw detail-page and verified-source captures are not included. See [Yearn evidence notes](./README.md).

## Executive summary

Fresh JOC captures show **32 vaults, 101 direct strategy associations (62 funded, 39 zero debt), five leveraged references across 6 vault-reference rows, and 96 distinct strategy URLs**. No association was added or removed from the previous audit; eight onchain debt values changed. The [Yearn vault catalog](https://yearn.fi/vaults) raw HTML includes 31 of 32 JOC vault addresses (Woofy is absent from that filtered catalog capture). [Inventory](./evidence/auctions/strategy_auction_inventory.csv) and [raw page hashes](./evidence/auctions/evidence.json) preserve every association.

**25 strategies have a nonzero auction endpoint now**, 34 have auction-capable source with a zero endpoint, and 37 have no auction route in their verified strategy source. The 25 configured strategies include 15 funded direct associations. They lead to 20 strategy-facing auction contracts; six Katana child auctions and four additional event-discovered external child auctions bring the shared-contract inventory to **30**. There are 33 currently enabled standard auction/token pairs, four immutable Flex sell tokens, and the middleman's two KAT/WKAT route tokens. [Auction contracts](./evidence/auctions/auction_contracts.csv) and [asset states](./evidence/auctions/auction_assets.csv) contain the full lists.

At the pinned blocks, the only identified nonzero direct auction custody is **40,976,114 raw USD3 (40.976114 tokens at 6 decimals) and 4,846,554,916,698,480,033,062 raw KAT (4,846.554916698480 tokens at 18 decimals)** across four inactive standard auctions. The other enabled pair balances and all Flex auction sell-token balances are zero. No enabled standard auction is active, and all 93 Flex IDs below the four DutchDesk nonces are inactive at their pinned block. These are reward/collateral inventories rather than vault deposit principal. No supported token/USD price snapshot was obtained, so this report gives raw and token amounts without USD totals. [Balances and live status](./evidence/auctions/auction_assets.csv).

## Auction mechanics, custody, and upgrade controls

The standard [Yearn auction implementation](./evidence/auctions/auction_contracts.csv) holds sell tokens after a strategy transfer; `kick` marks an enabled balance available, `take` transfers it to the buyer and pulls want tokens from the buyer to the configured receiver, `settle` closes an empty auction, and governance `sweep` moves any token to governance. `enable` excludes the want token. The auction grants max ERC-20 allowance on enabled tokens to the [CoW VaultRelayer](https://etherscan.io/address/0xC92E8bdf79f0507f65a392b0ab4667716BFE0110); its EIP-1271 order validation checks sell/buy tokens, price, receiver, amount and expiry. The relayer is deployed on Ethereum and Base and its immutable `onlyCreator` is the [settlement contract](https://etherscan.io/address/0x9008D19f58AAbD9eD0D60971565AA8510560ab41); read-only calls from settlement succeeded and an unrelated caller reverted. On Katana the same relayer address has **no code**, despite max allowance. This is a dormant deployment/control dependency, not proof that an EOA has its private key. [Code, allowances and simulations](./evidence/auctions/evidence.json).

The 29 standalone auction engines (25 standard and four Flex) have fixed EIP-1167 clone targets and zero EIP-1967 implementation/admin slots at the pinned block. The middleman is a direct contract. Their verified auction source has no upgrade entrypoint or mutable implementation address. Standard governance transfers are two-step (`transferGovernance` then `acceptGovernance`) and have no code delay; the middleman uses direct governance transfer. The relevant Safes require their onchain signature thresholds; no enabled Safe modules were returned by the paginated module getter. [Implementations and controllers](./evidence/auctions/auction_contracts.csv), [Safe reads](./evidence/auctions/auction_roles.csv).

On the standard auctions holding USD3 or KAT now, the 3-of-6 governance Safes can call `setReceiver` before an auction becomes active, redirecting future buyer proceeds; they can also set minimum/starting price and sweep held tokens directly. A `setReceiver` call is blocked while any auction on that contract is active. The three EOA-governed standard auction ABIs have no receiver setter, and all three currently hold zero enabled-token inventory. [Version-specific ABI and live role evidence](./evidence/auctions/auction_roles.csv).

The four [Flex auction engines](https://etherscan.io/address/0x9bF027D92DAfd0Aa8176eDC174922e22d1E67D56#code) have immutable buy/sell tokens. Only their DutchDesk (`papi`) can kick or re-kick; only the TroveManager can call DutchDesk.kick, and TroveManager redemption for Lender withdrawals requires Lender. DutchDesk's minimum buffer is 0.99 of the oracle price and its configuration has no setter. `re_kick` is permissionless but recalculates the same oracle-bounded price and cannot change the stored receiver. The [ExitRouter](https://etherscan.io/address/0xe8a511403B0C83e7b85513e00Ed39996B48c2aeD#code) can set the strategy's proceeds receiver only within a redeem call that burns the caller's vault shares and clears the receiver before returning. [DutchDesk, oracle and all 93 ID reads](./evidence/auctions/evidence.json).

The [Katana middleman](https://katanascan.com/address/0xA88D354A885FaDD27FB0E76E472256e3c13a265B#code) has ten event-reconciled registrants, including six JOC LP strategies. Its 3-of-6 Safe governance can replace assigned child auctions, change KAT campaign data, fee (up to 10%) and fee recipient, sync/wrap KAT, and sweep any held token to an arbitrary address. A strategy KAT kick currently reverts when the middleman has insufficient WKAT; a read-only call from a management Safe confirmed this for a strategy with KAT. Non-KAT token kicks forward to the assigned child auction. Child enabled KAT settings are distinct from the middleman's actual KAT campaign route. [Registry logs and mapping](./evidence/auctions/evidence.json).
